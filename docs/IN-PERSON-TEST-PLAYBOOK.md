# In-person test and content playbook

Use this playbook when testing Bwend with people you meet in real life or making short-form
content about those tests. The product is a private music companion for two people who already
chose to connect. Do not present it as a dating marketplace, compatibility verdict, or public
profile network.

## The two QR paths

1. **Private Bwend invite:** an approved Spotify tester creates an invite in Bwend and shows its
   QR on the iPhone. The other person scans it with the iPhone Camera app, connects Spotify,
   claims the one-time invite, and sees the private reveal. Afterward, either person can
   explicitly create a private Bwend playlist. If a Spotify Blend invite was attached, the same
   Bwend page also offers the intentional handoff to Spotify.
2. **Public interest:** if the person cannot join because Spotify access is limited, show the QR
   in the website's launch-list section. It opens
   `https://www.bwend.xyz/?interest=in_person#waitlist`. The person enters only an email and must
   actively consent to limited-beta and launch messages. The source records only
   `in_person_qr`—never a location, who they met, or their music taste.

The private QR is generated on the device/browser from the same seven-day, one-time Bwend URL.
It does not require camera permission on the showing phone. The receiving phone uses its normal
Camera app.

## A simple live conversation

Opening (about 10 seconds):

> We already met—want to see where our Spotify taste overlaps? It makes a private one-time link,
> not a dating profile.

Before scanning:

> You choose whether to connect Spotify. We see the music overlap only after both of us opt in,
> and either of us can delete our account or disconnect later.

After the reveal:

- Ask which shared artist or track surprised them.
- Open the anchor track in Spotify if both people want to listen.
- Offer the private playlist button; do not create it silently.
- If using Spotify Blend too, explain that Spotify—not Bwend—controls that Blend and may show
  Spotify usernames/profile pictures to its members.

If their Spotify account is not approved for the current beta, do not retry repeatedly. Show the
public interest QR and say:

> Spotify limits who can connect during this beta. If you want, you can leave an email for access
> or launch news; it is separate from Spotify and you can remove it immediately.

## Ethical short-form content

Product consent and filming consent are separate. Ask before recording, and ask again before
publishing. A Bwend signup never grants permission to use someone's face, voice, screen, name,
Spotify identity, or listening history in content.

Safe 30–45 second structure:

1. Hook: “Can two people tell if they vibe from the music they actually play?”
2. Show your own Bwend QR, without lingering on the invite code.
3. Cut away during Spotify authentication; never capture credentials, email addresses, tokens,
   or private notifications.
4. With both people's permission, show only the agreed reveal details.
5. Ask one music question: “What overlap surprised you?”
6. Close with the public interest QR and “Spotify access is limited while we test.”

For anyone who does not want to be identifiable, film only the tester's hands/phone, blur the
other screen, and paraphrase their feedback. Stop recording immediately if consent is withdrawn.
Keep signed media releases outside Bwend; do not attach them to Taste Cards or matches.

## What to record as research

Use aggregate, non-identifying counts:

- conversations started;
- private QR scans attempted;
- two-phone reveals completed;
- private playlists deliberately created;
- public interest QR scans and consented signups;
- where people became confused or abandoned the flow.

Do not write down names, handles, locations, inferred relationships, sensitive traits, or exact
listening histories. Quote feedback only with separate permission. The website stores the
launch-interest email, consent record, broad source, management-token hash, and two-year expiry;
it does not join that record to Spotify, Taste Card, or match data.

## Before each field session

- Confirm both test Spotify accounts are allowlisted.
- Install the latest iPhone build on both devices and test the universal link once.
- Create a fresh invite; never reuse an already-claimed code.
- Check the QR at normal arm's length and in the venue's lighting.
- Confirm the public interest QR opens the consent form.
- Have a non-recorded route for anyone who wants to try Bwend but not appear in content.
- Delete test accounts and content takes when requested.

After the session, add only aggregate outcomes and product issues to the beta log. Treat a failed
scan, Spotify quota denial, or refusal to be filmed as normal—not as pressure to collect more
information.
